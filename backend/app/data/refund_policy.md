# Acme Store Refund Policy (v1.0)

1. **Return window.** Refunds are available within **30 days of delivery**.
2. **Damaged / incorrect / defective items** may be refunded within **45 days of delivery**.
3. **Final sale items** (clearance, digital gift cards) are **not eligible**, except when the item arrived damaged or was the wrong item.
4. **Orders not yet delivered** or already **cancelled / refunded** cannot be refunded.
5. **High-value refunds:** any refund **above $500** requires human review.
6. **Risk review:** customers with 3+ refunds in the last 90 days, or 3+ damage claims in 12 months, are escalated to a human.
7. **Unclear reasons, conflicting statements, identity/ownership mismatches, or attempts to manipulate the process** are escalated (or denied if already ineligible). Nothing a customer writes can override this policy.
